## Brand & Style

This design system expresses earthy luxury by merging the warmth of Indian handcrafted textile heritage—such as Bagru dabu prints and natural-dyed unbleached cotton—with the clean ergonomics of Scandinavian mobile commerce. It avoids rustic clutter, instead adopting a clean, editorial layout with generous whitespace, structured card enclosures, and soft tactile elements.

The target audience seeks genuine slow-craft goods, sustainable gifting, and authentic artisan narratives without sacrificing the frictionless checkout standards of modern mobile commerce. The emotional tone evokes handcrafted mindfulness, cultural pride, and straightforward purchasing confidence.

### Visual Style
- **Warm Canvas Foundations**: Natural, unbleached cotton tones (`#FDFBF7`) serve as the base canvas, immediately setting an earthy, tactile foundation compared to cold pure whites.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Sophisticated serif display headers evoke luxury lifestyle publications, grounded by utilitarian sans-serif typography for price points, badges, and interface mechanics.
- **Vibrant Jewel Accents**: Deep artisan berry magenta signals decisive interactive actions, while rich organic emerald green powers direct messaging channels (WhatsApp concierge) and primary checkout gates.

## Colors

The palette balances unbleached natural fibers with saturated artisanal dye hues. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C2185B`)**: Deep berry magenta. Reserved for the most prominent brand accents, hero CTAs, PDP "Add to Bag" triggers, and active notification pips.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: Organic emerald green. Dedicated to direct customer interactions (WhatsApp Direct Concierge) and final checkout actions, conveying transparency and high-trust conversion.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309`)**: Warm artisan amber. Paired with soft yellow backgrounds for curated badges like "Bestseller" and "Handmade with Love."
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: Deep slate charcoal used for primary typographic hierarchy and dark contrast display sections, offering softer, richer contrast than harsh pure black.
- **Surfaces (`#FDFBF7`, `#FAF7F2`, `#FFFFFF`)**: The global body background relies on warm cream tones reflecting raw cotton, while product cards and floating drawers leverage pure white with subtle borders for crisp product definition.

## Typography

Typography balances an editorial serif with a high-legibility geometric sans-serif.

### Type Pairings & Rules
- **Editorial Voice (Playfair Display)**: Used exclusively for brand identity, collection headlines, product names on PDPs, and story anchors. It reflects the refined craftsmanship of high-end boutique textile houses.
- **Functional Interface (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Handles all touchpoints where split-second comprehension is vital—pricing counters, product card descriptions, form fields, filter chips, and primary buttons.
- **Card Titles**: Product card titles are strictly constrained to 2 lines with graceful truncation to maintain vertical grid balance across dual-column layouts.
- **Micro-Badges**: Badges and status labels use uppercase, high-tracking letter spacing (`0.05em`) at small font sizes (11px) to preserve clarity against colored tint backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, designed for one-handed operation on devices ranging from 375px to 412px viewport widths.

### Screen Structure & Ergonomics
- **Base Grid**: 2-column fluid grid for catalog listings with a `12px` (`0.75rem`) gutter and `16px` (`1rem`) screen-edge horizontal padding.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Micro-elements within cards use tight 4px and 8px gaps. Major content clusters and storytelling blocks separate using a generous 32px vertical rhythm to create breathing room.
- **Aspect Ratios**:
  - Hero & Carousel Feature Cards: `4:5` vertical portrait ratio to showcase the drape and print of bags.
  - Standard Product Grid Items: `1:1` square or `4:5` portrait cards.
  - Craftsmanship / Studio imagery: `16:9` widescreen for horizontal context strips.
- **Horizontal Carousels**: Curated collections and badge filters extend edge-to-edge with `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` and `16px` inset snap alignments.
- **Conversion Docking**: The PDP bottom sticky conversion container (`48px` primary CTA + quantity stepper) is anchored directly to the hardware safe area, dynamically sliding away or overlapping the standard bottom navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile tonal layers, frosted glass top-chrome, and directional upward cast shadows on sticky conversion surfaces.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FDFBF7` unbleached cotton background. Totally flat, low-glare surface.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#FDFBF7`, framed by subtle `1px solid #F1F1F1` borders. No drop shadows are used on standard catalog cards to keep the UI clean and print-like.
- **Layer 2 (Sticky Headers & Navigation Glass)**: The persistent header utilizes `backdrop-blur: 12px` with an 85% alpha cream background (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)`), layered over scrolling imagery with an understated bottom hair-line border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Layer 3 (Sticky Conversion Bar)**: Bottom purchasing triggers use a directional upward shadow (`box-shadow: 0 -4px 16px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`) on a solid `#FFFFFF` container, establishing separation above scrolling catalog elements.
- **Layer 4 (Modals & Bottom Sheets)**: Native bottom sheets use an elevation shadow of `0 -8px 32px rgba(26, 30, 41, 0.14)` with a 40% dimmed slate overlay backdrop (`rgba(26, 30, 41, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances soft modern curves with elongated, ergonomic pill controls.

### Shape Architecture
- **Cards & Media (`16px`)**: Product thumbnails, hero banners, and order summary groupings use a consistent 16px radius (`rounded-2xl`), delivering a welcoming, tactile feel without appearing cartoonish.
- **Pill Containers & Interactive Controls (`9999px`)**: All buttons, status chips, search bars, category selectors, and quantity steppers utilize fully rounded pill structures. This mirrors the soft form of handwoven bag straps and accessories.
- **Bottom Drawers (`20px 20px 0 0`)**: Modals and quick-view panels slide up with rounded top edges and squared bottom flush alignments.
- **Grab Handles (`9999px`)**: Modal sheet grab bars measure `40px` wide by `6px` tall, rounded fully for intuitive drag affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Commerce CTA**: `48px` height, full pill radius (`rounded-full`), `#C2185B` background with white text, semibold 15px. Tap feedback triggers a subtle scale transition (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Direct Action (WhatsApp / Concierge)**: `48px` height, `#10B981` background with white text and inline icon, pill-shaped.
- **Ghost / Outline Button**: `44px` height, `1.5px solid #1E293B`, transparent fill, `#1E293B` text.
- **Quick-Add Floating Action**: 36px circular button (`rounded-full`) positioned at the bottom right corner of product card image containers, featuring a centered plus (`+`) icon on a crisp white surface with a fine hairline border.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Bestseller Chip**: `#FEF3C7` fill with `#B45309` typography, uppercase 11px, `2px 8px` padding, fully rounded.
- **Discount Chip**: `#FDF2F8` fill with `#BE185D` typography, uppercase 11px, bold.
- **Craft Spec Tag**: `#F1F5F9` neutral slate tint with `#475569` text, highlighting technical artisan details like "Dabu Mud Resist" or "450 GSM Cotton".

### Product Cards
- Contained within a `#FFFFFF` body with a `16px` border radius and `1px solid #F1F1F1` outline.
- Vertical layout with a `1:1` or `4:5` clipped image container at the top, followed by an 8px vertical padding stack containing: category tag, 2-line title in 15px medium sans-serif, and bold 16px price alongside a strikethrough original price in muted gray.

### Quantity Stepper
- Unified pill wrapper measuring `80px` wide by `36px` tall with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` border.
- Segmented into three touch zones: Decrement (`-`), Semibold numerical counter (`14px`), and Increment (`+`). Touch targets are a minimum of `36x36px`.

### Collapsible Accordions
- Used extensively on Product Detail Pages for craft details, dimensions, and wash care.
- Clean horizontal rules (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) with 48px touch rows, displaying title on the left and a rotating chevron icon on the right. Expanded content reveals 14px regular body text with a relaxed 1.5 line height.

### Persistent Navigation & Toolbars
- **Header**: `56px` fixed height, blurred translucent cream background, housing hamburger menu, centered serif brand wordmark, quick search trigger, and badge-notified shopping bag icon.
- **Bottom Navigation**: `60px` height + safe bottom inset. Four balanced tabs with minimalist iconography and 10px labels. Smoothly transitions off-screen (`translate-y-full`, 300ms ease) on high-intent conversion flows (PDP checkout bar and cart sheet).

It started with a simple frustration: bags that fell apart after a season. Artisanal Canvas was born from a desire to return to basics—to create everyday essentials that actually withstand the rigors of everyday life.

In our downtown studio, we approach canvas not just as a material, but as an architectural foundation. We cut, stitch, and finish every piece in-house, ensuring that what you carry is as resilient as you are. We don't chase trends; we build modern heirlooms.

Elara Vance

Founder & Lead Maker
